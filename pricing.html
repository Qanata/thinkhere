Questions

Before you decide

Do I need an account to use ThinkHere?

No. Open thinkhere.ai in a supported browser and start chatting straight away. A free account unlocks all 8 models, image and audio upload, and conversation export.

Does upgrading change where my data goes?

No. Every plan runs the AI on your device. Your account only manages your settings and which features you can use. It plays no part in answering your questions.

Is ThinkHere really private?

Yes, and you don't have to take our word for it. The code is open source, and you can watch your browser's network activity while you chat. You will see no outbound requests during a conversation.

Who can read my conversations?

Nobody at Qanata Labs. Conversations are stored in your browser, on your device, and never reach our servers.

Why is there a download the first time?

The AI model has to live on your device to run there. The first load downloads it once. After that it opens from your browser's cache in seconds.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, anytime. You keep everything in the free account, and your conversations stay on your device.

Where can I find more help?

The Guide covers setup, models, device support and troubleshooting in detail.
